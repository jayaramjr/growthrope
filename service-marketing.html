How the work moves

From diagnose to optimize.

Marketing compounds when it's built on a clear diagnosis. We compress the loop down to four steps and repeat it relentlessly.

01

Diagnose

Audit of positioning, channels, funnel and message. We find where the gap actually is.

02

Plan

Positioning, message hierarchy, channel mix and a rolling 90-day campaign calendar.

03

Launch

Creative, copy and channels go live in coordinated waves — with tracking in place from day one.

04

Optimize

Weekly reads on what's working, then reallocation and iteration. Winners get scaled, losers get cut.
