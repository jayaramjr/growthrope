How the work moves

From discover to deliver.

Our design process has five deliberate stages. Each one ends with a decision, not a deliverable — so nothing is polished before it's proven.

01

Discover

User interviews, competitive review, and stakeholder alignment to understand what we're actually solving.

02

Define

We frame the problem, agree on the jobs-to-be-done, and set the principles that will guide every decision.

03

Design

Structure, flows and interfaces come together — first in low-fidelity, then in full-fidelity screens.

04

Refine

Prototypes get tested with real users. We iterate quickly, kill what doesn't work, and harden what does.

05

Deliver

Documented components, design tokens, and hand-off specs so engineering can build with confidence.
