How the work moves

From analyze to measure.

Five steps. One loop. Repeated monthly — so growth stops being an event and starts being a habit.

01

Analyze

Funnel mapping, cohort analysis and interviews to find where the business is actually losing value.

02

Strategize

Prioritised roadmap of experiments, each with a hypothesis, a metric and a target lift.

03

Experiment

Weekly tests shipped with proper tracking. Small, reversible, judged on evidence only.

04

Scale

Winners get rolled out across channels, segments and flows — with safeguards and monitoring.

05

Measure

Every cycle ends with a read: what we learned, what we're doubling down on, and what we're cutting.
